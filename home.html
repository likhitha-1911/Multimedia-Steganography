What is Steganography?

Steganography is the practice of concealing messages or information within other non-secret text or data. It allows secure communication by embedding encrypted data inside multimedia such as images, audio, or videos, making it invisible to the naked eye.

QuantumShield Pro combines classical encryption methods (AES, DES, RSA, ECC) with various steganographic techniques and includes support for quantum simulation-based data hiding, making it a future-ready platform.
